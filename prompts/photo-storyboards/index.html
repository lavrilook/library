Из одного фото — серия ракурсов

Два промпта: универсальная раскадровка и fashion-серия из 12 кадров.

Когда использовать

Когда есть исходное фото и нужно показать образ с разных расстояний и ракурсов.

Как пользоваться

Загрузи фото. Для универсального варианта заполни [number] и выбери формат. Для fashion-серии используй фото модели с товаром, детали которого хорошо видны.

Что получится

Серия согласованных кадров или сетка раскадровки. Выбранные кадры можно доработать отдельно.

Перед началом

Генерация может изменить лицо или конструкцию товара. Сверяй каждый результат с исходником; невидимые детали нужно предоставить отдельно.

Универсальная раскадровка

Use the uploaded image as the main visual reference. Create a cohesive storyboard series based on this image. Preserve the same person’s identity, facial features, hairstyle, outfit, styling, location, lighting, color palette, and overall visual mood unless otherwise specified. Create [number] different shots with varied poses, framing, camera angles, and distances. Include a mix of full-body, medium, close-up, seated, reclining, back view, movement, crouching, and leaning poses where appropriate. Keep the same person and styling consistent across all frames. High-quality editorial photography, realistic anatomy, natural posing, cohesive visual storytelling. Aspect ratio: [1:1 / 4:5 / 9:16 / 16:9]. Arrange the result as a storyboard grid. Add only small frame numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 inside each image for easy extraction. Do not add shot names, descriptions, captions, or any other text.

Один образ — 12 кадров

Use the provided reference image as the exact reference for the model, fashion item, styling, colors, materials, proportions, construction details, and overall visual mood. Create a clean 12-frame fashion storyboard / contact sheet in a 3 rows × 4 columns grid. Carefully analyze the fashion item and preserve its exact category, silhouette, cut, length, fit, color, fabric, construction, seams, trims, hardware, decorative elements, and all other visible details. Do not invent or add anything that is not present in the reference. All 12 frames must show the same woman, the same exact fashion item or complete look, and the same styling. Place the model in the same bright professional white photography studio across all frames: seamless clean white background, white or very light neutral floor, high-key soft diffused fashion lighting, bright neutral exposure, soft natural shadows, no visible architecture, beige walls, wooden floor, molding, furniture, or props. Keep the same face, hairstyle, body proportions, natural skin texture, expression, lighting direction, and realistic editorial aesthetic. The final image should look like a polished fashion lookbook collage: equal-size panels, thin white gutters, minimal clean layout, no captions or descriptive text, only small white numbers in the top-left corner of each frame: “01”–“12”. Adapt every shot intelligently to the garment: 01 — front full-length or medium-full shot showing the silhouette. 02 — 3/4 view showing fit and construction. 03 — side or alternative 3/4 view emphasizing shape. 04 — back view showing rear construction. 05 — medium or close fashion portrait highlighting the strongest visual area. 06 — close-up of one important construction or design detail. 07 — close-up of another distinctive garment detail. 08 — detail shot showing fabric behavior, hem, edge, drape, layering, or interaction with the body. 09 — close-up of another important material or construction detail. 10 — medium side or 3/4 editorial shot showing how the item sits on the body. 11 — full-length shot showing complete silhouette and proportions. 12 — final medium fashion shot highlighting the strongest feature. Choose close-ups only from details actually present in the reference: waistband, pockets, seams, hem, neckline, waist, draping, straps, sleeves, back construction, collar, buttons, fastenings, cuffs, shoulders, fabric texture, or other relevant visible features. Automatically adapt framing and close-ups to the specific garment type. Style: photorealistic, bright professional studio fashion photography, high realism, natural skin texture, realistic fabric texture, accurate stitching and garment construction, soft diffused high-key light, clean white background, minimal shadows, premium editorial and e-commerce look. Important: maintain maximum consistency across all 12 frames. Same model, garment, colors, materials, proportions, styling, white studio, lighting, and color grading. Do not redesign or alter the garment’s cut, construction, color, material, length, proportions, or visible details. No extra props or accessories unless already present in the reference. No extra text except the frame numbers.
